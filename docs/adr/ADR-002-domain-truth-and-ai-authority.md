# ADR-002 — Domain Truth and AI Authority

**Status:** Accepted

- Operational domains own canonical state.
- Intelligence consumes authorized facts/evidence and creates analysis, simulations and recommendations.
- AI inference cannot silently become verified truth.
- Recommendation is not a binding decision.
- Regulated, material or safety-relevant actions require the authority class defined by policy.

This prevents model output from becoming an uncontrolled transactional authority.
